Eatery — Help & support

Questions, problems with a booking, or something to report? Email andresinp@gmail.com. We answer within two working days, and review safety reports within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I pay? You pay a small booking deposit in the app; the rest goes to the host in person.

Can I cancel? Yes, from the booking. At least 24 hours before, the deposit is refunded.

Where is the meal? The exact address appears in your booking once it is confirmed.

How do I report someone? Open their profile, the listing or the chat, tap ⋯ and choose Report.
