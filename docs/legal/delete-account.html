Delete your Eatery account

In the app: open Eatery → Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. Upcoming bookings are cancelled and deposits refunded, then your profile, photos, listings, bookings, messages and notifications are deleted permanently.

Can't open the app? Email andresinp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Eatery account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
