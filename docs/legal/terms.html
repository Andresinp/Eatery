What Eatery is

Eatery connects people who cook at home ("hosts") with people who want to eat with them or buy their food ("guests"). Hosts are independent individuals, not Eatery employees. The meal or food is provided by the host, not by Eatery.

Bookings and payments

When you book, you pay a booking deposit in the app. The rest of the price is paid directly to the host in person. Guests who cancel at least 24 hours before the meal or pickup get the deposit back; later cancellations and no-shows do not. If a host cancels or does not show up, the guest's deposit is refunded in full.

Hosts

Hosts are responsible for the food they serve: describing it honestly, listing every allergen, handling it safely and following the food-safety, tax and local rules that apply to them. Allergen suggestions in the app are help, not a guarantee; the host must check them.

Guests

Check the allergen information and ask the host if you are unsure. Treat hosts and their homes with respect.

Community rules

No harassment, discrimination, unsafe or illegal food, misleading listings, or asking to pay outside Eatery. Report anything that breaks these rules from the listing, profile or chat. We review every report within 24 hours and may remove content or accounts.
