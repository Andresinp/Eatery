Eatery — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Eatery ("we") is an app for booking seats at home-cooked meals and ordering homemade food from people near you. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: andresinp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored hashed), or the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google and the email address they share.
  • Profile: your name, an optional photo and bio, and your ratings and reviews.
  • Dietary preferences and allergens to avoid (optional). We use them only to filter listings and warn you about food that contains them. Nobody else can see them.
  • Listings you host: photos, descriptions, prices, times, the area shown on the map, and your exact address, which is shown only to guests who have booked.
  • Bookings and payments: what you booked, when, how many, and the deposit paid. Card details are entered into and held by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Location: while you use the map, if you allow it, to show food near you; when you host, to place your listing. We do not track your location in the background or store it with your profile.
  • Messages you exchange with a host or guest about a booking.
  • Reports and blocks you make, so we can keep the community safe.
  • Push token, if you allow notifications.

How we use it

Only to run Eatery: to sign you in, show listings, take booking deposits and refunds, share the meeting address with the people involved, deliver messages, reminders and notifications, and review reports. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see what

Your name, photo, bio, ratings and reviews are public to other Eatery users. A host's area (not exact address) is public on listings; the exact address goes only to guests with a confirmed booking. Messages are visible only to the guest and host of that booking. Your email, dietary preferences and allergens are never shown to anyone.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, file storage and sign-in hosting.
  • Stripe — card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments and refunds.
  • Anthropic — when a host asks for help, the listing's title and description are sent to suggest a description and possible allergens. No personal data is included.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, and push notifications (via the Expo push service).
  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles (your device requests map images; no account data is sent).

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Upcoming bookings are cancelled and deposits refunded, then your profile, photos, listings, bookings, messages and notifications are permanently deleted. If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account. Payment records Stripe must keep by law stay with Stripe.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw permissions (location, photos, camera, notifications) in your phone's settings at any time. To make a request, email us.

Age

Eatery is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
